# Changelog

Your AI reads this at every monthly look-back and proposes at most one thing.

## v0.4 (draft, 14 October 2026)
- **No character.** Packmate is retired: the person's own AI is the only one in any chat, and
  the instructions it follows are plain skills, `setup` and `teach`. Exercise notes are now
  "For the AI running this".
- **Wired in, not visited.** Setting up ends by connecting every app the person uses, each at
  a level: wired, connected or a copy, recorded in FLEET.md and shown on the dashboard.
- **House rules travel.** START.md and every copy carry five house-rule lines and an order of
  priority: what the person says right now beats everything; the rucksack beats an app's own
  memory, which may be stale.
- **How I like to learn**, a new section in LEARN.md, asked while setting up and followed by
  every app.
- **The look-back catches up the month**: it asks each app what it remembers and brings it
  back into the rucksack.
- **The dashboard** (`view.html`) in six plain sections: Status, Learning, Skills and tools,
  Notes, Try things, Health check. A `try/` scratch folder.
- **connectors.md**: ChatGPT and Claude phone apps read and write a rucksack through their
  GitHub connectors (tested 14 October 2026).
- For an existing rucksack: the upgrade replaces AGENTS.md, the tidy and the three skills
  (`skills/packmate/` becomes `skills/setup/` and `skills/teach/`), and adds `try/`. It never
  touches your own files: FLEET.md keeps its old columns until the next look-back offers the
  new ones, and LEARN.md gets "How I like to learn" by proposal.

## v0.3 (draft, 11 October 2026)
- The six dimensions are now called **the six areas** (owner ruling), so they never clash
  with `skills/`, the person's own skills.
- First twelve exercises, E01 to E12: levels 1 and 2, written as rucksack builds. Each
  one leaves something in the rucksack and has a check the person sees pass.
- connectors.md: the Codex app is now the ChatGPT desktop app (Mac, Windows, Linux) with
  Chat, Work and Codex; Claude's folder work (Cowork) needs a Pro or Max plan.
- For an existing rucksack: nothing to change. Packmate will start offering these
  exercises at the next session.
