# E06 · Talk instead of type

level 1 · area: Checking · about 15 minutes · access: an app with voice input (optional)

## The idea
Voice input is quick, but transcription gets names, numbers and the word "not" wrong more
often than you would think.

## Why it matters to you
If you would rather talk than type, this tells you what to check before acting on what it
heard.

## Do it
1. Dictate a harmless set of notes that includes a name, a number and a "do not".
2. Ask for an action list from them.
3. Compare the action list with what you said, line by line: the name, the number, the
   "do not".

## What goes in your rucksack
- A line in `FLEET.md` under How the work is divided: when you use voice, and what you
  always check afterwards.

## The check
You checked the name, the number and the negative against what you actually said, and
corrected anything wrong.

## For the AI running this
Optional. Typing is a full alternative; offer it without fuss if voice is awkward or
inaccessible. Evidence for Checking level 1.
