# E09 · Write your SOUL from real corrections

level 2 · area: Asking · about 25 minutes · access: any chat app

## The idea
The best instructions for how an AI should behave come from the corrections you have
actually made, not from a template.

## Why it matters to you
Every correction you put in `SOUL.md` is one you never have to make again, in any app.

## Do it
1. Do a real piece of work with AI (a draft, a plan). Ask for three changes one at a
   time, each naming a specific problem: "too long", "too formal", "you invented a date".
2. Look at your three corrections. Which ones would you make every time?
3. Turn each into one line for `SOUL.md`, in your words.

## What goes in your rucksack
- Up to three new lines in `SOUL.md` (proposed, then applied with your yes), and one line
  under "Never do this with me" if a correction belongs there.
- The finished piece of work, wherever it lives.

## The check
Open a fresh chat with your rucksack and do a similar task. None of the three problems
comes back. If one does, the line was not clear enough; rewrite it.

## For the AI running this
Evidence for Asking level 2. Keep SOUL about how the AI behaves; facts about the person
belong in `USER.md`.
