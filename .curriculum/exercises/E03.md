# E03 · Same question, three answers

level 1 · area: Checking · about 10 minutes · access: any chat app

## The idea
An AI can give a different answer each time you ask the same thing. One good answer does
not prove the next one will be good.

## Why it matters to you
It tells you what you have to check every time, rather than trusting a tool because it
was right once.

## Do it
1. Take a harmless request built on notes you already know well (a shopping list from a
   recipe, a summary of a page you wrote).
2. Ask it three times, each in a fresh chat.
3. Compare: what changed, what went missing, what was added that you never gave it.

## What goes in your rucksack
- A short "always check" list. Propose it as a line for `USER.md` under How I work:
  `Always: check <the thing that varied> before I use it.`
- Under `## Remember` in today's note: what varied.

## The check
Every fact you know should be there is checked across all three answers, and the
differences are written down. Three identical answers is a valid result, not a failure.

## For the AI running this
Evidence for Checking level 1. Use the person's own material; never invent a document for
this one, because they cannot check what they do not know.
