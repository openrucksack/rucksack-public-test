# E02 · Vague ask, clear ask

level 1 · area: Asking · about 15 minutes · access: any chat app

## The idea
An AI only knows what you give it. A clear ask has five parts: the goal, who it is for,
the facts it needs, any limits, and the shape you want back.

## Why it matters to you
The difference between a generic answer and a useful one is usually the ask, not the AI.
You will reuse a good ask many times.

## Do it
1. Take a real task (yours from E01 is fine). Ask for it in one vague line and keep the
   answer.
2. Ask again in a fresh chat with the five parts: goal, audience, facts, limits, form.
3. Compare both answers against your success criteria.

## What goes in your rucksack
- The five-part ask, saved in `projects/<name>.md` under a heading `Brief`, with any
  real details replaced by placeholders like [client] or [date].
- Under `## Remember` in today's note: which of the five parts made the biggest
  difference.

## The check
You can name at least one concrete improvement the clear ask produced. If there was no
difference, you revise the brief once and say what you changed.

## For the AI running this
Evidence for Asking level 1 (or level 2 if they did it without help). The brief becomes a
skill in E11; tell them that now so they keep it.
