# Exercises

One file per build. Each has: the idea, why it matters to the person, the steps, what goes
in the rucksack, the check that proves it worked, and notes for the AI running it. Access needed is
on the second line of each file.

| ID | Build | Level | Area | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E01 | Pick a job worth doing | 1 | Choosing | 15 min |
| E02 | Vague ask, clear ask | 1 | Asking | 15 min |
| E03 | Same question, three answers | 1 | Checking | 10 min |
| E04 | When the answer is not there | 1 | Checking | 15 min |
| E05 | What goes in, what stays out | 1 | Staying safe | 20 min |
| E06 | Talk instead of type (optional) | 1 | Checking | 15 min |
| E07 | A summary you can trust | 1 | Checking | 20 min |
| E08 | Does it know you somewhere else? | 1 | Keeping it running | 15 min |
| E09 | Write your SOUL from real corrections | 2 | Asking | 25 min |
| E10 | Which model for what | 2 | Choosing | 30 min |
| E11 | Your first skill | 2 | Keeping it running | 25 min |
| E12 | A second opinion is not a check | 2 | Checking | 25 min |

Levels 3 to 5 (E13 to E42) follow after the pilot shows whether this shape works. No one
is meant to do these in order; the AI offers at most two that fit what the person has
been doing.
