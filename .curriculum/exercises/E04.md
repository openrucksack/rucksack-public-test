# E04 · When the answer is not there

level 1 · area: Checking · about 15 minutes · access: any chat app

## The idea
When an AI does not have a fact, it may make one up in a confident voice. The fix is to
ask where an answer came from, and to tell it to say "not in what you gave me".

## Why it matters to you
This is the habit that catches the most expensive mistakes.

## Do it
1. Ask your AI for a short invented document: a one-page event notice, say, with the
   venue left out on purpose.
2. In a fresh chat, give it the document and ask for the venue, and where in the document
   it found it.
3. See what happens. Then ask the same with this added: "If it is not in the document,
   say so."

## What goes in your rucksack
- A rule, proposed for `SOUL.md`: "If it is not in what I gave you, say so. Never fill a
  gap with a guess."
- Under `## Remember`: what the AI did the first time.

## The check
You found out whether the first answer was supported by the document. A correct "it is
not there" counts as a pass; so does catching an invented venue.

## For the AI running this
Evidence for Checking level 1. The invented document is deliberate; say so. Do not try to
force a wrong answer; record what happened either way.
