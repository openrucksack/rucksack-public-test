# E07 · A summary you can trust

level 1 · area: Checking · about 20 minutes · access: any chat app

## The idea
A summary can quietly add things that were never in the original, or drop the one point
that mattered.

## Why it matters to you
Summaries are one of the most common uses of AI. Knowing how to check one makes them
safe to rely on.

## Do it
1. Pick a short document you understand well and are happy to share: a public article,
   your own notes, a page of instructions.
2. Ask for five points, each with where it came from in the document.
3. Trace every point back to the document. Mark anything added and anything important
   left out.

## What goes in your rucksack
- The checked summary, with your corrections, in `projects/` or as a plain file.
- Under `## Remember`: what it added or missed, if anything.

## The check
Each of the five points traces to the document. Additions are removed or labelled;
important omissions are put back.

## For the AI running this
Evidence for Checking level 1, or level 2 if they ask for sources without prompting.
Keep it to a document they know; that is what makes the check possible.
