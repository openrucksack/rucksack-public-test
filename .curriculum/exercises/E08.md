# E08 · Does it know you somewhere else?

level 1 · area: Keeping it running · about 15 minutes · access: a second app, or a fresh chat

## The idea
Your rucksack is only useful if it travels. The test is simple: open it somewhere new and
see whether that AI knows you.

## Why it matters to you
This is the moment the rucksack stops being a folder and starts being yours across every
app.

## Do it
1. Write down three things any AI working for you should know (from `USER.md`).
2. Open your rucksack in a second app, or a fresh chat in the same app. If that app cannot
   read the rucksack, use pack-for to make a paste-ready copy and paste it in.
3. Ask that AI the three things. Do not hint.

## What goes in your rucksack
- A row in `FLEET.md` for the second app: reads the rucksack, writes to it, or holds a
  paste copy from today's date.

## The check
The second AI answers all three correctly from your rucksack, not from you. If it gets
one wrong, fix the line in `USER.md` or `START.md`'s source that should have told it.

## For the AI running this
Evidence for Keeping it running level 1, and the first proof of portability that level 5
builds on. The `connectors.md` table says which apps can read a rucksack directly this
month.
