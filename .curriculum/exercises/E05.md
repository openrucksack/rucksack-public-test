# E05 · What goes in, what stays out

level 1 · area: Staying safe · about 20 minutes · access: any chat app, plus the app's settings

## The idea
What you type goes to the company that runs the app, under its settings. Some things are
fine to share, some need permission or editing first, and some never go in.

## Why it matters to you
It is the one lesson that protects other people as well as you, and it makes your
rucksack's boundary something you have thought about rather than guessed.

## Do it
1. Your AI gives you ten invented snippets (a client's name and balance, a recipe, a
   colleague's phone number, your own goals, a password, and so on).
2. Sort each one: fine to share; needs permission or editing first; never.
3. Open the settings of the AI app you use most and find two things: whether your chats
   are used to train its models, and how to delete a chat. Change what you want changed.

## What goes in your rucksack
- Your boundary in `USER.md`, sharpened by what the sort taught you.
- A line in `FLEET.md` for that app: the training setting you chose and the date.

## The check
You can explain each of the ten choices, and your app setting now matches what you
decided. If you work for someone, your choices match their policy, or you have noted
that there is none.

## For the AI running this
Evidence for Staying safe level 1. Every snippet is invented; no real data in this
exercise, ever. If the person cannot find the setting, help them search the app's help
pages rather than guessing where it is.
