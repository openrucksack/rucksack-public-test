# E11 · Your first skill

level 2 · area: Keeping it running · about 25 minutes · access: an app that can write files to your rucksack

## The idea
A skill is a reusable instruction your AI can pick up whenever the job comes round, in any
app that reads the skills standard. It is the brief from E02, grown up.

## Why it matters to you
It turns "explain it again every time" into "do the usual".

## Do it
1. Take a brief you have used at least twice (from E02, or any task you repeat).
2. With your AI, turn it into `skills/<name>/SKILL.md`: a short name, one line saying
   when to use it, then the steps, the checks, and what to do when something is missing.
3. Open a fresh chat and ask for the job in a few words, without pasting the brief.

## What goes in your rucksack
- `skills/<name>/SKILL.md`, listed automatically in `SKILLS.md` at the next tidy.
- Under `## Done` in `LEARN.md`: the skill, the date, and the fresh-chat check.

## The check
In the fresh chat the AI finds and follows the skill, and the result meets your criteria
without you re-explaining. If it does not find it, the one-line description needs to
say more clearly when to use it.

## For the AI running this
Evidence for Keeping it running level 2. The name is lowercase with hyphens and matches
the folder. No real details in a skill; placeholders only.
