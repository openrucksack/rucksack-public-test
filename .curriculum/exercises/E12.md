# E12 · A second opinion is not a check

level 2 · area: Checking · about 25 minutes · access: any chat app, plus the web

## The idea
Asking an AI to check its own work, or another AI's, is not the same as checking it
against the evidence. A confident critique can miss what matters.

## Why it matters to you
It stops you outsourcing the one job that has to stay yours: deciding whether something
is true before you act on it.

## Do it
1. Your AI gives you a short, harmless draft with three mistakes in it, and keeps the
   answer key.
2. Ask an AI to review the draft for errors.
3. Check the draft yourself against the answer key. Which mistakes did the review find,
   and which did it miss?
4. Then, on a real piece of your own work, pick three facts that matter and check each one
   against an original source you open yourself.

## What goes in your rucksack
- A claim ledger in `learn/evidence/claim-ledger-<date>.md`: each claim, the source you
  checked, the date, and whether it held.
- A line proposed for `SOUL.md`: "Show me the source for any fact I am going to rely on."

## The check
All three planted mistakes are corrected, whether or not the review caught them, and each
real claim in your ledger has a source you opened yourself.

## For the AI running this
Evidence for Checking level 2. The planted draft is invented; say so. The real claims use
the person's own work, with nothing from their boundary written into the ledger.
