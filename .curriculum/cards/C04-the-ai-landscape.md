# The AI landscape

![Four kinds of AI tool, from least to most independent: chat assistants, AI inside your tools, agents that do a job, personal agents; models underneath them all](C04-the-ai-landscape.svg)

**The idea.** AI tools differ mostly in how much they do on their own. Four kinds, from least to
most independent:

- **Chat assistants:** you ask, it answers. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot,
  Perplexity.

- **AI inside your tools:** help where you already work. Copilot in Word, Excel and Outlook;
  Gemini in Gmail and Docs; Notion AI.

- **Agents that do a job:** you hand over a task; they work through your files and apps and hand
  back finished work. ChatGPT Work, Claude Cowork; for code, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor.

- **Personal agents:** always on, with their own computer: you message them, they act across your
  accounts and ask before anything big. Meta Muse, ChatGPT Dots, Gemini Spark.

Underneath them all are **models**, the part that thinks (OpenAI's GPT, Anthropic's Claude,
Google's Gemini). They change every month or two, which is why your rucksack keeps your context
outside any one of them.

**In your setup.** You listed the AI tools you use, and what you use each for.

**Check it landed.** "Which kind is the tool you use most, and which kind haven't you tried?"

**What proves it.** Their tools are listed in their rucksack, with what each is for.

**For the AI.** Checked 15 October 2026; the field moves monthly, so add anything newer you know,
and say so. Also: Codex lives inside the ChatGPT desktop app (with Chat and Work) since July
2026; Cursor is now owned by SpaceX, alongside Grok; "Copilot" is three products (the Microsoft
Copilot app, Copilot in Microsoft 365 apps, GitHub Copilot for code); ChatGPT Dots is on paid
plans only; OpenClaw is an open-source personal agent people run themselves, powerful but not for
beginners. Other chat assistants: Grok, Meta AI, DeepSeek. Relate it to them: which kinds they use,
which they don't, and one worth trying for something they told you they do. Diagram, to draw only
if asked: four boxes left to right from least to most independent, with their examples, and a bar
under them labelled "models, changing every month or two".
