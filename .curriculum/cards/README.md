# Cards

Short explainers, one idea each, that your AI offers at the moment the idea comes up. Each has a
diagram, about a hundred plain words, one question to check it landed, and the can-do it adds to
your learning profile once you've done the thing it explains.

| Card | When it comes up | Area |
|---|---|---|
| [C00 What Rucksack is](C00-what-rucksack-is.md) | Any time someone asks what it is or how it works | (none) |
| [C01 GitHub and privacy](C01-github-and-privacy.md) | Making your rucksack | Staying safe |
| [C02 Connectors and permissions](C02-connectors-and-permissions.md) | Connecting an AI tool | Keeping it running |
| [C03 What a new chat starts with](C03-what-a-new-chat-starts-with.md) | After the new-chat check works | Asking |
| [C04 The AI landscape](C04-the-ai-landscape.md) | Listing the AI tools you use | Choosing |

**For the AI using these:** the facts here were checked on the date in each card. Explain them in
your own words, at the person's pace and the way they like to learn, and keep to these facts. If
they want more, go deeper from what you know, and say so when you're less sure or something may
have changed since the card was checked. Link the card, which GitHub displays with its
picture on any device; only draw the diagram yourself (each card describes it) if they ask, as
drawing is slow on a phone.
