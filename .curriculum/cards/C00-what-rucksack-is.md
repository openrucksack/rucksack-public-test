# What Rucksack is

![Without Rucksack, each AI app keeps its own slice of you. With it, the apps you connect read one private set of files you own](C00-what-rucksack-is.svg)

**The idea.** Each AI app keeps its own memory of you, so ChatGPT, Claude and the rest each know a
different slice, and you explain yourself again in every app you open. Rucksack is one private
set of plain files about you (who you are, how you like AI to work with you, the tools you use,
what's worth remembering, where you are with AI) that the AI apps you connect read before they
answer. Think of it as custom instructions, but yours, and the same in every app. You can read
every line your AI reads. And because your AI knows your work and your level, it can help you get
better at AI on the things you actually do.

**Where it lives.** In your own GitHub account. We never see it; only you and the apps you connect
can read it.

**For the AI.** Use these words, not your own label for it ("operating manual", "second brain" and
"memory layer" are not ours). Say "your rucksack" first, then "your pack" is fine. There is no
character or second assistant in it: you are their AI. Be exact about how each app reaches it:
apps that open the folder, like Claude Code or Codex in the ChatGPT desktop app, read it at the
start of every chat; ChatGPT and Claude with the line in their settings open it at the start of a
chat through the GitHub connection; any other app gets a copy they paste in, which can't save
back. Diagram, to draw only if asked: on the left, three apps each with its own small memory; on
the right, the same apps reading one rucksack the person owns.
